# ISO/IEC 27001 — Lesson 2

## Risk Assessment
Risk assessment identifies information security risks and evaluates their likelihood and impact.

**Simple formula:**
Risk ≈ Likelihood × Impact

## Risk Treatment
- **Mitigate:** Reduce risk using security controls.
- **Share/Transfer:** Share some consequences with another party.
- **Avoid:** Stop the activity creating the risk.
- **Accept:** Formally retain a risk within approved criteria.

## Annex A
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A contains 93 reference security controls grouped into four themes:

- Organizational: 37 controls
- People: 8 controls
- Physical: 14 controls
- Technological: 34 controls

## Statement of Applicability (SoA)
The SoA records which Annex A controls are applicable, whether they are implemented, and the reasons for including or excluding them.

## Cloud Security Example
Risk: An attacker compromises an AWS account and accesses customer data.

Treatment:
- Enable MFA.
- Apply least-privilege IAM permissions.
- Encrypt sensitive data.
- Enable and review CloudTrail logging.

## Key Takeaway
ISO 27001 connects risk assessment to security controls. Organizations select appropriate controls, document their applicability, and review their effectiveness.
