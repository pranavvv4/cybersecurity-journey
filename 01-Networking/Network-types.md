# 🌐 Network Types

Networks can be classified based on their size, coverage area, and purpose.

---

## 1. PAN — Personal Area Network

A Personal Area Network (PAN) is a small network used around an individual.

### Examples

- Smartphone connected to wireless earbuds
- Phone connected to a smartwatch
- Bluetooth keyboard connected to a laptop

### Characteristics

- Very small range
- Usually used by one person
- Commonly uses Bluetooth
- Connects personal devices

---

## 2. LAN — Local Area Network

A Local Area Network (LAN) connects devices within a limited geographical area.

### Examples

- Home network
- School network
- Office network
- Computer laboratory

### Characteristics

- Covers a small geographical area
- Usually privately managed
- Provides high-speed communication
- Connects multiple devices within the same local area

---

## 3. MAN — Metropolitan Area Network

A Metropolitan Area Network (MAN) covers a larger area than a LAN, typically across a city or metropolitan region.

### Examples

- University campuses connected across a city
- Company offices connected within a city
- City-wide networks

### Characteristics

- Larger coverage than a LAN
- Usually covers a city or metropolitan area
- Can connect multiple LANs
- Uses high-speed communication links

---

## 4. WAN — Wide Area Network

A Wide Area Network (WAN) connects networks across large geographical areas.

### Examples

- Company networks connecting different cities
- Bank networks connecting branches across a country
- Networks connecting different countries
- The Internet

### Characteristics

- Covers a very large geographical area
- Can connect cities, countries, or continents
- Connects multiple LANs and MANs
- Often uses telecommunications infrastructure

---

## 5. WLAN — Wireless Local Area Network

A Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN) is a LAN that uses wireless communication, usually through Wi-Fi.

### Examples

- Home Wi-Fi network
- College Wi-Fi
- Office Wi-Fi
- Public Wi-Fi

### Characteristics

- Uses wireless communication
- Usually uses Wi-Fi
- Allows devices to connect without Ethernet cables
- Covers a limited geographical area

---

## Quick Summary

| Type | Full Form | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| PAN | Personal Area Network | Personal space |
| LAN | Local Area Network | Building / local area |
| MAN | Metropolitan Area Network | City |
| WAN | Wide Area Network | Large geographical area |
| WLAN | Wireless Local Area Network | Wireless local area |
