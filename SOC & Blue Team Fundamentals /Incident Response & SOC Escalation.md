# 🚨 Incident Response & SOC Escalation

Incident response is the structured process of handling security incidents, while escalation transfers an issue to a team with the appropriate authority or expertise.

## Examples

```text
Security Alert
      ↓
Initial Investigation
      ↓
Incident Confirmed or Suspected
      ↓
Containment
      ↓
Eradication
      ↓
Recovery
      ↓
Lessons Learned
```

Escalation example:

```text
SOC L1 → Initial Triage
SOC L2 → Deeper Investigation
SOC L3 → Advanced Analysis & Detection Engineering
Incident Response Team → Containment & Recovery Coordination
```

## Characteristics

- **Containment** → Limit the spread or impact of a threat
- **Eradication** → Remove malicious activity and its root cause
- **Recovery** → Restore systems safely and monitor for recurrence
- **Escalation** → Transfer complex, high-risk, or confirmed incidents to the appropriate team
- **Evidence Preservation** → Retain relevant logs and evidence according to procedures
- **Communication** → Notify relevant stakeholders according to incident severity and policy
- **Lessons Learned** → Improve detections, controls, and response procedures

## Key Takeaway

**Incident Response → Contain, eradicate, recover, and improve.**

**SOC Escalation → Send incidents to the right team with clear evidence and documented findings.**
