# 🎯 Threat Intelligence & MITRE ATT&CK

Threat intelligence is information about threats, attackers, their capabilities, and their methods. MITRE ATT&CK is a knowledge base that organizes observed adversary tactics and techniques.

## Examples

Common threat intelligence sources:

- CISA advisories
- Vendor threat reports
- Open-source threat intelligence feeds
- MITRE ATT&CK

Example attack sequence:

```text
Phishing Email
      ↓
Malicious File Executed
      ↓
Credential Access
      ↓
Lateral Movement
      ↓
Data Exfiltration
```

MITRE ATT&CK tactics describe an attacker's goals, while techniques describe how those goals may be achieved.

## Characteristics

- **Threat Intelligence** → Helps security teams understand threats and make informed decisions
- **Tactics** → The adversary's objectives, such as Initial Access or Credential Access
- **Techniques** → Methods used to achieve those objectives
- **Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)** → Artifacts such as suspicious IP addresses, domains, or file hashes
- **Threat Hunting** → Proactively searches for malicious activity that may have gone undetected
- **ATT&CK Mapping** → Helps analysts classify observed behavior and identify detection gaps

## Key Takeaway

**Threat Intelligence → Understand threats and their capabilities.**

**MITRE ATT&CK → Organizes adversary tactics and techniques to support detection, investigation, and defense.**
