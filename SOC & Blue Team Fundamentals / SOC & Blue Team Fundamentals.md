# 🛡️ SOC & Blue Team Fundamentals

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a team or function that monitors, detects, investigates, and responds to cybersecurity threats. Blue Team refers to the defensive security personnel and activities that protect an organization's systems and data.

## Examples

```text
Security Events
      ↓
Monitoring & Detection
      ↓
Alert Triage
      ↓
Investigation
      ↓
Incident Response
      ↓
Recovery & Improvement
```

## Characteristics

- **SOC** → Monitors and investigates potential security incidents
- **Blue Team** → Defends systems, networks, applications, and data
- **Security Analyst** → Reviews alerts and investigates suspicious activity
- **Security Engineer** → Builds, configures, and improves security controls
- **Threat Detection** → Identifies potentially malicious activity
- **Incident Response** → Contains threats and supports recovery

## Key Takeaway

**SOC → Detect, investigate, and respond to threats.**

**Blue Team → Defend systems and continuously improve security.**
