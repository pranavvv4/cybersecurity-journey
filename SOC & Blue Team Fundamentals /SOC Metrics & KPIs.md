# 📊 SOC Metrics & KPIs

SOC metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) help measure how effectively a Security Operations Center detects, investigates, and responds to security threats.

## Examples

```text
MTTD → Mean Time to Detect
MTTR → Mean Time to Respond or Recover
Alert Volume → Number of alerts received
False Positive Rate → Percentage of alerts incorrectly identified as threats
Incident Resolution Time → Time taken to resolve incidents
```

Example:

```text
Incident Occurred → 10:00 AM
Threat Detected   → 10:05 AM
Response Started  → 10:10 AM

Time to Detect    → 5 minutes
Time to Respond   → 10 minutes
```

## Characteristics

- **MTTD** → Measures the average time taken to detect a threat or incident
- **MTTR** → Can mean Mean Time to Respond or Mean Time to Recover; organizations should define which meaning they use
- **Alert Volume** → Helps track monitoring workload and trends
- **False Positive Rate** → Measures how often alerts incorrectly indicate malicious activity
- **Incident Resolution Time** → Tracks how long incidents take to resolve
- **Reporting** → Communicates trends, response effectiveness, risks, and areas for improvement

## Key Takeaway

**SOC Metrics → Measure detection speed, response effectiveness, alert quality, and incident-handling performance.**
