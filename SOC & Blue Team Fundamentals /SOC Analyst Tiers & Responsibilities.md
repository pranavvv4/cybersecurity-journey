# 👥 SOC Analyst Tiers & Responsibilities

SOC analysts monitor security activity, investigate alerts, and respond to potential threats. Their responsibilities vary depending on experience, team structure, and organizational needs.

## Examples

```text
SOC Tier 1 → Alert Monitoring & Initial Triage
      ↓
SOC Tier 2 → Detailed Investigation & Incident Analysis
      ↓
SOC Tier 3 → Advanced Threat Hunting & Detection
      ↓
Incident Response / Security Engineering
```

## Characteristics

- **Tier 1 (L1)** → Monitors alerts, performs initial triage, documents findings, and escalates suspicious activity
- **Tier 2 (L2)** → Investigates incidents in depth, correlates evidence, assesses scope, and recommends containment
- **Tier 3 (L3)** → Handles complex investigations, advanced threat hunting, malware analysis, and detection improvements
- **SOC Manager** → Oversees operations, staffing, processes, and reporting
- **Incident Response Team** → Coordinates containment, eradication, and recovery
- **Security Engineer** → Builds and improves security tools, detections, and controls

## Key Takeaway

**L1 → Triage.**

**L2 → Investigate.**

**L3 → Advanced analysis and detection improvement.**

*These tiers are a common model, not a universal standard; responsibilities vary between organizations.*
