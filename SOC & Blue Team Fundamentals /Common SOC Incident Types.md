# 🚨 Common SOC Incident Types

## Definition
SOC Incident Types are categories of security incidents that SOC analysts identify, investigate, and respond to when an organization's systems, networks, or data are at risk.

## Examples
- **Malware Infection:** A system is infected with malicious software.
- **Phishing Attack:** An attacker tricks users into revealing sensitive information.
- **Brute-Force Attack:** Repeated login attempts are used to guess passwords.
- **Ransomware Attack:** Malware encrypts files and demands payment.
- **Account Compromise:** An unauthorized person gains access to a user account.
- **Insider Threat:** An employee or trusted user misuses authorized access.
- **Data Breach:** Sensitive information is accessed or exposed without authorization.
- **Denial-of-Service (DoS):** An attacker disrupts a service by overwhelming it with traffic.

## Characteristics
- **Unauthorized Access:** Attackers access systems or accounts without permission.
- **Data Theft:** Confidential information may be stolen or exposed.
- **System Disruption:** Services or business operations may become unavailable.
- **Malicious Activity:** Attackers use malware, scripts, or other techniques.
- **Investigation Required:** Analysts collect evidence to understand the incident.
- **Response Required:** Incidents may require containment, eradication, and recovery.

## Important Details

| Incident Type | Common Warning Sign |
|---|---|
| Malware | Suspicious processes or files |
| Phishing | Fake emails or suspicious links |
| Brute-force | Repeated failed logins |
| Ransomware | Files suddenly become encrypted |
| Account compromise | Unusual login or account activity |
| Insider threat | Unusual access to sensitive data |
| Data breach | Unauthorized data access or transfer |
| DoS | Unusual traffic and service disruption |

## Key Takeaway
SOC analysts must recognize different incident types, investigate their indicators, determine their impact, and follow the appropriate incident response process.
