# 🧮 Risk Assessment

Risk assessment is the process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating risks to determine which ones require attention.

## Examples

A simple risk calculation:

```text
Risk = Likelihood × Impact
```

Example:

```text
Threat       → Ransomware
Likelihood   → High
Impact       → High
Risk         → High
```

## Characteristics

- Identifies potential security risks
- Evaluates likelihood and impact
- Helps prioritize security efforts
- High-risk issues should generally receive higher priority
- Risk assessments support security decision-making

## Key Takeaway

**Risk Assessment → Determine how likely a security event is and how serious its impact could be.**
