# ⚠️ Threats, Vulnerabilities & Risk

A threat is a potential cause of harm, a vulnerability is a weakness that can be exploited, and risk is the potential impact of that exploitation.

## Examples

```text
Threat
  ↓
Vulnerability
  ↓
Exploitation
  ↓
Impact
  ↓
Risk
```

Example:

```text
Threat → Attacker
Vulnerability → Weak password
Exploitation → Password attack
Impact → Unauthorized account access
Risk → Data compromise
```

## Characteristics

- **Threat** → Potential source of harm
- **Vulnerability** → Weakness in a system or process
- **Exploit** → Method used to take advantage of a vulnerability
- **Risk** → Potential loss or damage resulting from a threat exploiting a vulnerability
- Reducing vulnerabilities can reduce security risk

## Key Takeaway

**Threat → Potential danger**  
**Vulnerability → Weakness**  
**Exploit → Takes advantage of the weakness**  
**Risk → Potential impact**
