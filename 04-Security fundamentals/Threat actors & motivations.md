# 🎭 Threat Actors & Motivations

Threat actors are individuals or groups that intentionally or unintentionally pose a security threat to systems, networks, or data.

## Examples

```text
Cybercriminal → Financial gain
Nation-state   → Espionage / strategic goals
Insider        → Misuse of authorized access
Hacktivist     → Political or social causes
Script kiddie  → Learning / experimentation
```

## Characteristics

- **Cybercriminals** → Usually motivated by financial gain
- **Nation-state actors** → Often target intelligence or strategic information
- **Insiders** → Misuse legitimate access
- **Hacktivists** → Attack for political or social causes
- **Script kiddies** → Use existing tools or exploits without deep technical knowledge
- Motivations help security teams understand potential attacker behavior

## Key Takeaway

**Threat Actor → Person or group that can cause or attempt to cause security harm.**
