# 🕵️ Indicators of Compromise & Attack

Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) and Indicators of Attack (IOAs) are clues used to identify malicious activity or potential attacks.

## Examples

```text
IOC → Malicious IP address
IOC → Suspicious file hash
IOC → Known malicious domain

IOA → Repeated failed logins
IOA → Unusual PowerShell execution
IOA → Unexpected privilege escalation
```

## Characteristics

- **IOC** → Evidence that a compromise may have occurred
- **IOA** → Evidence of attacker behavior or activity
- IOCs are often specific artifacts
- IOAs focus more on attacker actions and behavior
- Used in threat detection and incident investigation

## Key Takeaway

**IOC → Evidence of compromise.**  
**IOA → Evidence of attack behavior.**
