# 🎣 Phishing & Social Engineering

Phishing is a cyberattack that uses deceptive messages or websites to trick people into revealing information or performing unsafe actions. Social engineering is the broader manipulation of people to bypass security.

## Examples

```text
Phishing          → Fake email requesting a password
Spear Phishing    → Targeted phishing against a specific person
Smishing          → Phishing through SMS
Vishing           → Phishing through voice calls
Pretexting        → Using a fabricated story to gain trust
Baiting           → Offering something tempting to trick a victim
```

## Characteristics

- Exploits human trust, fear, urgency, or curiosity
- May imitate legitimate organizations or people
- Can steal credentials or deliver malware
- Suspicious links, unexpected attachments, and urgent requests are warning signs
- Verification through a trusted channel helps prevent attacks
- MFA can reduce risk but does not prevent every phishing attack

## Key Takeaway

**Phishing → Deceptive messages used to trick victims.**

**Social Engineering → Manipulating people to gain access or information.**
