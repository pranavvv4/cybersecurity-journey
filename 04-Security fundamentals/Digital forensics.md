# 🕵️ Digital Forensics

Digital forensics is the process of collecting, preserving, examining, and analyzing digital evidence to investigate security incidents.

## Examples

```text
Identification
      ↓
Collection
      ↓
Preservation
      ↓
Examination
      ↓
Analysis
      ↓
Reporting
```

Common evidence sources:

- System and security logs
- Disk images and files
- Network traffic captures
- Browser artifacts
- Memory captures

## Characteristics

- Preserves evidence for investigation
- Uses methods that maintain evidence integrity
- Records how evidence was collected and handled
- Uses hashes to help verify evidence has not changed
- Maintains a chain of custody
- Supports incident response and legal investigations

## Key Takeaway

**Digital Forensics → Collecting and analyzing digital evidence while preserving its integrity and documenting its handling.**
